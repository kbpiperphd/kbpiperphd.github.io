Katherine is a 3rd year PhD Student in Computational Mechanics at Carnegie Mellon University in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Her research interests are at the intersection of computational algorithm development and experiment. Recent results include models of thin-film evaporation in porous wicks, which enabled a 42% efficiency improvement in Loop Heat Pipes, predictive models for fracture propagation in ductile functionally graded alloys, and nonlinear reduced order models development with strong mathematical guarantees.
